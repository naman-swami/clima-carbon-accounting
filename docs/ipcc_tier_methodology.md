# GHG Protocol Corporate Standard Reference

## Scopes Definition (ISO 14064 / GHG Protocol)
- **Scope 1**: Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources owned or controlled by the enterprise (furnaces, company vehicles).
- **Scope 2**: Indirect greenhouse gas emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, or cooling.
- **Scope 3**: Value chain emissions, including upstream and downstream transportation, business travel, and product use.
